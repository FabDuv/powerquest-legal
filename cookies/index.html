Déposés uniquement après acceptation via le bandeau :

Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This page describes the trackers used by the PowerQuest web app. The iOS app does not use web cookies; its advertising is governed by Apple's transparency framework (ATT) and Google AdMob.

1. Strictly necessary storage (no consent required)

Exempt from consent (Art. 82 French Data Protection Act / ePrivacy Directive) as it is essential to the service:

ItemTypePurposeDuration
Supabase sessionLocal storage / cookieKeep you signed inSession
pq_ad_consentLocal storageRemember your ad choiceUntil withdrawn
pq_legalConsentAtLocal storageProof of legal consent (age, health)Until account deletion
pq_onboardingDoneLocal storageAvoid re-showing the welcome screenPersistent

2. Advertising cookies (consent required)

Set only after acceptance via the banner:

ProviderPurposeLearn more
Google AdSenseServing and measuring adspolicies.google.com
  • Before any choice: no advertising script or targeting cookie is loaded.
  • If you accept: personalised ads may be shown.
  • If you decline: non-personalised ads may be shown, without a targeting cookie (npa=1 parameter).

3. Manage or withdraw your consent

You can change your choice at any time: Profile → "Manage ad choices" → the banner reappears and lets you change your decision (consent is as easy to withdraw as to give, per Art. 7.3 GDPR).
